Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

What we store

  • On your device: your favorites, starred series, races marked Going/Watched, time zone and settings, saved in your browser so the app remembers them.
  • If you create an account: your email address, display name, those same preferences, and your prediction picks. Picks and display names are visible to other signed-in players on the leaderboard.
  • If you turn on alerts: a push address from your browser and which series and alert types you chose, so we can send notifications. Turning alerts off deletes it.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, and there's no advertising tracking. We don't ask for your location; times use your phone's time zone setting.

Services we use

Hosting by GitHub Pages; accounts, picks and alert settings stored with Supabase; fonts from Google Fonts; some photos loaded from Wikipedia; news headlines link to the original publishers. Links to tickets, streaming services and official sites take you to those companies' sites, which have their own policies.

Your choices

You can sign out, turn alerts off, or clear the app's data in your browser at any time. To delete your account and everything linked to it, email contact@premierracinginfo.com from the address you signed up with.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change too.
