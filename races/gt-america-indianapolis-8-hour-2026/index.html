Indianapolis 8 Hour 2026

GT World Challenge America · Indianapolis Motor Speedway, Indianapolis, IN · Oct 9–11, 2026 · 8 hours, Finale

Results

GMG Racing back-timed its last stops and won by 56.209s, Porsche's first Indy 8 Hour win and the first by a Pro-Am crew. Foley passed Schandorff's Ferrari for the Pro lead with an hour left, which decided the Pro title. The last 5 hours 39 minutes ran without a caution.
