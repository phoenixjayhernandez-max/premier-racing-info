F1 Sim Racing World Championship

F1 25 · Mar 27 – May 28, 2026 · 12 races at 4 live events · Finished

Formula 1's official esports championship. The ten F1 teams field sim racers, with a $750,000 prize fund paid to the teams.

  • Drivers' champion: Otis Lawrence, Alpine Sim Racing · 156 pts
  • Teams' champion: Oracle Red Bull Sim Racing, 227 pts

Lawrence became the youngest champion in the series, two points ahead of Ferrari's Ismael Fahssi. 2027 season not announced yet.

eNASCAR Coca-Cola iRacing Series

iRacing · May 19 – Oct 14, 2026 · 13 races · Finished

NASCAR's official sim racing series, with real Cup teams and a playoff that ends in a Championship 4 race.

  • Champion: Steven Wilson, Spire Motorsports · his third title
  • Team champion: Spire Motorsports

Wilson beat Connor Yeroschak, Bobby Zalenski and Wyatt Tinsley in the Homestead-Miami finale, raced at the NASCAR Hall of Fame. Back for its 18th season in 2027.

Porsche Esports Supercup

iRacing · Sep 19 – Oct 24, 2026 · 5 rounds · Finished

Porsche's global sim championship in the 911 GT3 Cup. Regional series feed a five-round World Championship with a sprint and a main race each round.

  • Champion: Florian Lebigre, Verstappen Sim Racing · 276 pts

Lebigre won the Monza finale to beat Mitchell deJong by 9 points. Charlie Collins was third. 2027 plans not announced yet.

Race it yourself: the official games

  • F1 25 (EA Sports): The official F1 game, with a 2026 season expansion. Used for the F1 Sim Racing World Championship. Real series: Formula 1.
  • Assetto Corsa Competizione (Kunos Simulazioni): The official GT World Challenge game: GT3 cars, tracks and team liveries. Real series: GT Europe, GT America.
  • Le Mans Ultimate (Studio 397): The official game of the FIA World Endurance Championship, with Hypercars, LMGT3 and the 24 Hours of Le Mans. Real series: FIA WEC.
  • iRacing (Online subscription): Home of eNASCAR, the Porsche Esports Supercup and big special events like the Daytona 24. Real NASCAR, IMSA and Porsche cars. Real series: NASCAR Cup, IMSA WeatherTech, Supercup.
  • Gran Turismo 7 (Polyphony Digital): PlayStation's driving game and the platform for the Gran Turismo World Series.

Not running in 2026: the Esports World Cup dropped sim racing for Trackmania, and the Le Mans Virtual Series hasn't announced a new season.
